TechCorp Annual Report 2024
TechCorp Inc. was founded by Sarah Chen and Michael Rodriguez in 2015
in San Francisco, California. The company specializes in artificial
intelligence and cloud computing solutions for enterprise clients.
In January 2024, TechCorp acquired DataFlow Systems, a startup based
in Austin, Texas, for $450 million. DataFlow was founded by James
Park in 2019 and specializes in real-time data pipeline technology.
Sarah Chen serves as CEO of TechCorp, while Michael Rodriguez is the
Chief Technology Officer. After the acquisition, James Park joined
TechCorp as Vice President of Data Engineering.
TechCorp reported annual revenue of $2.8 billion in 2024, a 35%
increase from $2.07 billion in 2023. The company employs 12,000
people across 15 offices worldwide.
The flagship product CloudMind AI is used by over 500 Fortune 1000
companies. CloudMind AI uses a proprietary large language model called
TechLM-7B trained on enterprise data with customer permission.

Partnerships and Technology Stack
TechCorp has strategic partnerships with Amazon Web Services (AWS),
Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform.
In March 2024, TechCorp partnered with NVIDIA to integrate H100 GPUs
into CloudMind AI, resulting in 3x faster inference speeds.
NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang praised the partnership at GTC 2024.
Main competitors include Palantir Technologies, Snowflake, and
Databricks. TechCorp differentiates through vertical integration of
AI models with data pipeline technology after the DataFlow acquisition.
The engineering team led by CTO Michael Rodriguez uses Python and
Rust for backend, React for frontend, PostgreSQL and Apache Kafka
for data infrastructure, and Kubernetes for orchestration.
Board of Directors: Sarah Chen (Chair), Michael Rodriguez,
Lisa Wang (former CEO of Dropbox), Robert Kim (Sequoia Capital),
and Dr. Amy Foster (Stanford University).

Future Outlook and Strategy
TechCorp plans European expansion in 2025 with new HQ in London.
Maria Garcia hired as VP European Operations, previously Regional
Director at Salesforce.
R&D is developing TechLM-70B, launching Q3 2025, trained using
10,000 NVIDIA H100 GPUs hosted on AWS.
Investors: Sequoia Capital (Series A), Andreessen Horowitz (Series B),
SoftBank Vision Fund (Series C at $500M). IPO on NASDAQ Sep 2023,
ticker TCAI.
Key risks: competition from OpenAI and Anthropic in enterprise AI,
EU AI Act regulatory changes, and NVIDIA hardware dependency.
Mission: To democratize enterprise AI and make intelligent data
processing accessible to every organization.
